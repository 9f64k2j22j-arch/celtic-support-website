What we do

Everyday support, shaped around you.

We focus on practical, non-clinical supports that promote choice, participation and independence.

Types of support

Flexible help for everyday life.

01

Community participation

Support to attend activities, appointments, events and places that matter to you.

02

Social support

Company and practical assistance to build connections, pursue interests and participate in the community.

03

Daily routines

General assistance with agreed everyday tasks that do not require clinical or specialist qualifications.

04

Transport assistance

Agreed help getting to activities or appointments, where this is suitable and included in the service arrangement.

05

Short-notice support

Responsive support for unexpected needs, subject to worker availability and prior service arrangements.

06

Finding the right worker

Help considering workers whose availability, experience and approach may be a good fit.

Scope of service

We do not currently offer clinical care, high-intensity supports, supported independent living, regulated restrictive practices or services requiring mandatory provider registration. Some activities may still require specific checks, training, licences or experience. We confirm suitability before agreeing to deliver support.

Pricing

Clear and agreed before support begins.

Pricing within applicable NDIS limits

Our price for each support will be discussed and recorded in a service agreement. Where an NDIS price limit applies, our charge will not exceed the applicable maximum in the current official NDIS pricing arrangements or pricing schedule.

Prices may change when the NDIA updates its official arrangements. We will discuss proposed changes with existing participants before they apply. The NDIA sets maximum limits for many supports; it does not set Celtic Support’s individual prices.

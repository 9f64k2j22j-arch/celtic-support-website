Work with us

Good support begins with good people.

We want to hear from reliable, respectful people who want to make a difference and understand that every person is different.

Who we’re looking for

Character and capability both matter.

We want people from all walks of life, and we believe the right attitude is what matters. Depending on your experience—whether lived experience or on-the-job experience—we will aim to pair you with the right participant.

We also understand that everyone needs to start somewhere, and we want to support the beginning of your journey. If you want to learn and you value safety, respect and reliability, let us help you take that first step.

Depending on the role, requirements may include an NDIS Worker Screening Check, Working with Children Check, first aid, driver’s licence, insurance, references or role-specific training. We will confirm requirements before work begins.

Ways of working

Enjoy flexible pathways.

Option one

PAYG

Opportunities may be offered through casual employment, with the applicable employment conditions, supervision and support.

Option two

Independent contracting

Some genuinely independent arrangements may suit workers operating their own business. The correct arrangement depends on the real working relationship and applicable law—not preference or a label alone.

Our approach

Whichever pathway is suitable, we will explain the proposed arrangement clearly. We will not ask a worker to operate as a contractor where the role is legally an employment relationship.

NDIS advocacy

Clear support when the process feels difficult.

Independent, practical help to understand decisions, organise relevant information and prepare your next steps.

How we may help

Practical preparation, centred on you.

01

Understanding decisions

Work through a decision and identify the issues or questions that matter to you.

02

Internal reviews

Help organise your concerns, evidence and requested outcome for an internal review.

03

Change of circumstances

Structure information about significant changes and consider practical next steps.

04

Evidence review

Review documents for clarity, relevance and possible information gaps.

05

ART preparation

Non-legal assistance organising issues and documents for an Administrative Review Tribunal process.

06

Strategy sessions

A focused conversation about your situation, options and immediate priorities.

Our boundaries

Honest about what we can—and cannot—do.

Celtic Support is independent. We are not the NDIA, the NDIS Commission, the ART or a government agency. We do not control decisions and cannot guarantee an outcome.

Our advocacy service is non-legal support and does not replace advice from a lawyer or community legal centre. We may suggest specialist legal advice where appropriate. Review and appeal time limits may apply, so consider seeking help promptly.

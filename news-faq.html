Frequently asked questions

Things you may want to know.

Do you provide registered or clinical supports?

Our current focus is general, non-clinical support. We do not currently offer supported independent living, high-intensity supports, regulated restrictive practices or other services that require mandatory provider registration. We check each proposed service before agreeing to deliver it.

Do support workers need qualifications?

Not every general support role requires a formal qualification. Workers must still have the ability to deliver the agreed support safely and may need screening, licences, first aid, references or role-specific training.

How much does support cost?

Pricing is agreed before support begins. Where an NDIS price limit applies, charges will not exceed the applicable maximum in the current official pricing arrangements. See the What We Do page for more information.

Can you guarantee a particular worker or shift?

No. Availability and suitability can change. We communicate the available options clearly, but cannot guarantee a particular match or shift.

Is short-notice support an emergency service?

No. Short-notice support is subject to availability and prior arrangements. Call Triple Zero (000) in an emergency.

Are workers employees or contractors?

We are considering both models where legally appropriate. The arrangement will depend on how the work actually operates, applicable law and the particular opportunity.

Are you part of the NDIA?

No. Celtic Support is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NDIA, NDIS Commission or Australian Government.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Advocacy is non-legal practical support. We may recommend speaking with a qualified lawyer or community legal centre where appropriate.

About us

A more personal way to organise support.

Celtic Support was created to make finding and arranging everyday disability support feel simpler and more human. We take time to understand the participant, not just the hours that need filling.

Our planned services focus on general, non-specialist supports that can be delivered safely without clinical qualifications. Every arrangement will still reflect the worker’s skills, appropriate checks, the participant’s needs and current NDIS requirements.
